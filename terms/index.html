Terms of use

Last updated 13 October 2026 · Pilot version: to be reviewed by a lawyer before public launch.

These terms apply to ordering through Food Porter during the Tilburg pilot.

1. What we do

Food Porter connects you with independent Tilburg restaurants and local riders. The restaurant prepares your food and is responsible for its quality, ingredients and allergen information. Independent riders bid a delivery time and deliver your order.

2. Ordering & prices

  • Prices shown include VAT. A flat delivery fee applies (currently €2.50–€3.50); riders keep 100% of it.
  • We deliver only within the pilot zone (currently 15 km around central Tilburg; the exact radius is shown at checkout). Addresses outside the zone can’t be ordered to.
  • Your order is confirmed when payment succeeds via Mollie (iDEAL or card).

3. Delivery time

The ETA you see is the rider’s own estimate. We track how often riders deliver on time, but an ETA is not a guarantee.

4. Cancellations & refunds

  • If the restaurant rejects your order or doesn’t confirm it within 5 minutes, it is cancelled and you get a full automatic refund.
  • If no rider can be found, our team contacts you and refunds in full if we cancel.
  • Once the restaurant has started preparing, you can’t cancel (food is perishable; the right of withdrawal doesn’t apply, art. 6:230p BW). Problems with your order? Contact us within 24 hours and we’ll make it right.
  • Refunds go back to your original payment method, usually within 1–3 working days.

5. Alcohol & age

Alcohol is not sold during the pilot.

6. Your account

Keep your login safe. We may block accounts that misuse the service (e.g. fake orders, abuse of riders or restaurant staff).

7. Liability

Our liability is limited to the amount of the order concerned, except in case of intent or gross negligence. Dutch law applies.

8. Contact

support@foodporter.nl (pilot contact, to be confirmed). Company details (KvK number, address) will be added before public launch.
