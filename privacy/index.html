Privacy policy

Last updated 14 October 2026 · Pilot version: to be reviewed by a lawyer before public launch.

Food Porter (“we”) runs a food delivery pilot in Tilburg, the Netherlands. This policy explains which personal data we process, why, and your rights under the GDPR (AVG).

What we collect

  • Account: email address, name (optional), role, and sign-in data from Google/Apple if you use them.
  • Orders: delivery address and its map coordinates, phone number, items, amounts, timestamps, ratings and comments.
  • Payments: processed by Mollie B.V. We store the payment id and status, never your card or bank details.
  • Riders: name, phone, IBAN (for payouts), bids, delivery times and earnings.
  • Rider location (GPS): only after the rider consents, and only while they are online or on a delivery: position, accuracy, speed and heading, about every 7 seconds.
  • Restaurants: business contact details, menu and order handling times.

Why (legal basis)

  • To deliver your order and send status SMS: performance of a contract.
  • To pay riders and restaurants, keep accounting records (7 years) and prevent fraud: legal obligation / legitimate interest.
  • To measure and improve the service (e.g. % of orders delivered on time): legitimate interest, using aggregated data.

Live location

Riders choose when to share: going online starts sharing, going offline (with no delivery in progress) stops it. While a delivery is active, its customer and restaurant can see the rider on a map; after delivery they can no longer see it. Our admins can see online riders for dispatch and safety. Nobody else can see locations (enforced in the database). Location points are automatically deleted after 7 days. Legal basis: the rider’s consent (withdraw any time by going offline) and, during a delivery, performance of the contract with the customer. Maps use OpenStreetMap tiles (OpenStreetMap Foundation), which receive your IP address when the map loads.

Who we share it with

The restaurant and the assigned rider see what they need for your order (items, address, phone). Processors: Supabase (database & login, EU region), Mollie (payments, NL), PDOK/Kadaster (address lookup, NL), OpenStreetMap (map tiles), our SMS provider (Twilio or Bird) and GitHub Pages (hosting the app files). We do not sell data or use advertising trackers.

How long

Order and payment records: 7 years (Dutch tax law). Account data: until you delete your account. Ratings: until you ask us to remove them. Rider GPS points: 7 days.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data, object to processing, and complain to the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens. Download your data below; for deletion or questions contact us at privacy@foodporter.nl (pilot contact, to be confirmed). We reply within 30 days.
