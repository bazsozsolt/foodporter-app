English summary. Rider onboarding checklist: ID check (in person or by video), right to work, age (16+ employee, 18+ zzp), KvK/BTW for zzp, licence and insurance for scooters, signed contract, code of conduct and transparency notice, safety briefing and equipment, IBAN and payroll form. The rider can only go online when everything is done; reminders go out 30 days before documents expire.
