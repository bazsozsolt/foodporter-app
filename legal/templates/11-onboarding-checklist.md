---
**English summary.** Rider onboarding checklist (direct employment): ID check (in person or by video), right to work, age 16+, licence and insurance for scooters, signed employment contract, code of conduct and transparency notice, safety briefing and equipment, payroll tax form (loonheffingskorting), BSN entered via the payroll provider (the app only stores an 'entered' flag), IBAN check. No document copies in the app. The rider can only go online when everything is done; reminders go out 30 days before documents expire.
