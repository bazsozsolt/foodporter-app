English summary. An updated privacy statement covering customers, riders and restaurants: data, purpose, legal basis and retention per category. GPS is processed while the rider is clocked in and deleted after 7 days (DPIA). Photo proof is kept for 30 days and contract acceptance logs for the relationship plus 5 years. Rider working-time data (availability, clock-in/out, hours, breaks, Working Hours Act alerts) is processed for payroll and compliance and kept 7 years; BSN only at the payroll provider. Sub-processors: Supabase, Mollie, GitHub Pages and the push services. No deactivation without human review. Data-subject rights and a complaint to the AP.
