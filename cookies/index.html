Cookie policy

Last updated 13 October 2026 · Pilot version: to be reviewed by a lawyer before public launch.

Food Porter uses no tracking or advertising cookies and no third-party analytics, so we don’t show a cookie banner.

What we store on your device

  • Login session (browser local storage, set by Supabase Auth): keeps you signed in. Strictly necessary.
  • Your cart (local storage): remembers what you picked. Strictly necessary.
  • Alert preference (local storage, restaurants/riders): whether sound alerts are on.
  • Offline cache (service worker): app files, so the app opens fast and works as an installed app.

During payment you are redirected to Mollie, which uses its own strictly necessary cookies (see mollie.com/privacy).

You can clear all of this at any time in your browser settings; you will be signed out and your cart emptied.
