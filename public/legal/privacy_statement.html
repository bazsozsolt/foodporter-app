English summary. An updated privacy statement covering customers, riders and restaurants: data, purpose, legal basis and retention per category. GPS is processed while the rider is clocked in and deleted after 7 days (DPIA). Support tickets (with messages and photos) are deleted automatically 2 years after closing and order chat 6 months after the order is completed. Photo proof is kept for 30 days and contract acceptance logs for the relationship plus 5 years. Rider working-time data (availability, clock-in/out, hours, breaks, Working Hours Act alerts) is processed for payroll and compliance and kept 7 years; BSN, tax credit, payslips and the ID copy only at the payroll provider, which receives weekly hours, guarantees and surcharges as an export. Pension fund and occupational health service are separate controllers. Sub-processors: Supabase, Mollie, GitHub Pages and the push services. No deactivation without human review. Data-subject rights and a complaint to the AP.
